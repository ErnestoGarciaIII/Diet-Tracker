PlatePilot started as a simple idea: make eating well less complicated, more personal, and actually enjoyable.

Our Mission

At PlatePilot, we strive to build a user-friendly experience that helps you track and plan your daily nutrient intake with precision. Our goal is to alleviate health issues caused by malnutrition by providing the tools you need to take total control of your nutrition.

By leveraging the USDA FoodData Central database, our platform identifies select food items that fill the nutritional gaps in your current diet. Whether you have specific dietary restrictions or unique flavor preferences, our team combines expertise in database management and web development to bring your body's "co-pilot" to life.

How it Works

Precision on Every Plate

1

Profile Sync

We analyze your personal metrics and dietary goals to calculate your ideal micro and macro nutrient needs.

2

Data-Driven Analysis

Our system cross-references your intake with the USDA FoodData Central database to identify specific nutritional gaps.

3

Smart Recommendations

PlatePilot acts as your body’s co-pilot, suggesting select food items tailored to your allergies and flavor preferences.
