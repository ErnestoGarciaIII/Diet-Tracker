Your Privacy

Your Control.

Effective Date: April 13, 2026

1. Introduction

Welcome to PlatePilot! We are committed to protecting your personal information and your right to privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, and safeguard your data when you use our web application to track and plan your daily nutrient intake.

2. Information We Collect

To provide a personalized "co-pilot" experience for your nutrition, we collect the following information:

  • Account Information: Name, email, and login credentials stored in our localized database.
  • Health Metrics: Information you provide such as age, weight, height, activity level, and specific nutritional goals.
  • Preferences: Information regarding allergies, dietary restrictions, and flavor preferences.
  • Nutritional Logs: Data regarding the food items you track and your progress toward micronutrient and macronutrient goals.

3. How We Use Your Information

PlatePilot uses your data to:

  • Calculate your personalized recommended needs for micronutrients and macronutrients.
  • Generate smart food recommendations that fill nutritional gaps using the USDA FoodData Central database.
  • Maintain a record of your history to track progress toward your dieting goals.
  • Ensure the security and authentication of your account.

4. Data Storage and Security

We use Flask libraries to encrypt sensitive information in the database rather than storing it as plain text.

5. Third-Party Data

PlatePilot leverages the USDA FoodData Central database to provide accurate nutritional information. While we retrieve data from this public source, we do not share your personal health metrics or identity with the USDA or any other external third parties for marketing purposes.

6. Your Rights and Choices

You have the right to access, update, or delete your personal information at any time through your account settings. As an application built with a focus on user control, we ensure you remain the primary pilot of your data.
